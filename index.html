About
Where I sit

I work at the intersection of science and commercial strategy in life sciences, helping pharma and biotech organisations make smarter decisions on where to compete, who to partner with, and how to position their assets.

My background starts in the lab — five years investigating kinetochore biology for my PhD at the Max Planck Institute, preceded by research on monoclonal antibody purification at IIT Delhi and microscopy image analysis at EMBL Heidelberg.

I moved into consulting to apply scientific thinking to commercial questions. At Eradigm Consulting, I deliver competitive intelligence and strategic advisory across oncology, neurology, immunology, and rare diseases — translating complex market dynamics into actionable intelligence for pharma and biotech clients.

What drives me now are the forces reshaping this industry — whether that's AI restructuring how drugs are discovered and developed, upcoming regulatory milestones that will define therapeutic categories, or the shifting dynamics of how companies compete and partner. I write about these topics publicly, build tools to track the landscape, and try to make complex industry shifts more accessible.
